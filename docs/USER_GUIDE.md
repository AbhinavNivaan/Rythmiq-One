# Rythmiq One - User Guide

Welcome to Rythmiq One! This guide will help you get the most out of the app.

---

## 📱 Getting Started

### Download & Install

1. Download Rythmiq One from the App Store
2. Open the app and allow camera permissions when prompted
3. Enter your phone number to create an account
4. Verify with the OTP sent to your phone

### First Launch

When you first open the app, you'll see a quick tutorial showing:
- How to capture documents
- How to create master documents
- How to adapt for exam portals

You can skip this and access it later from Settings.

---

## 📸 Capturing Documents

### Taking a Photo

1. Tap the **Camera** tab at the bottom
2. Position your photo/document in the frame
3. Ensure good lighting (no harsh shadows)
4. Keep the camera steady
5. Tap the **capture button** to take the photo

### Tips for Best Results

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|-------|---------|
| Use natural daylight | Take photos in dim light |
| Hold camera parallel to document | Tilt at an angle |
| Fill most of the frame | Leave too much background |
| Use a plain background | Use cluttered backgrounds |
| Keep camera steady | Move while capturing |

### Automatic Enhancement

After capture, Rythmiq One automatically:
- Corrects brightness and contrast
- Removes minor noise
- Adjusts color balance
- Optimizes for crisp edges

## 🗄️ Your Document Vault

### Viewing Documents

1. Tap the **Vault** tab to see all your documents
2. Documents are shown as preview thumbnails
3. Tap any document to view details

### Document Types

You can store different document types:
- **Photo** - Passport-style photos
- **Signature** - Your signature
- **ID Document** - Aadhar, PAN, etc.
- **Certificate** - Mark sheets, degrees

### Managing Documents

**To rename a document:**
1. Open the document
2. Tap the edit icon next to the name
3. Enter new name and save

**To delete a document:**
1. Long-press on the document thumbnail
2. Tap "Delete" in the menu
3. Confirm deletion

⚠️ **Note:** Deleted documents cannot be recovered

## 🎯 Adapting for Exam Portals

This is where Rythmiq One saves you hours of work!

### Starting Adaptation

1. Open any document from your Vault
2. Tap **"Export for Portal"**
3. Select your target exam portal from the list
4. Wait for processing (usually 5-15 seconds)
5. Download the adapted file

### Supported Portals

Rythmiq One supports major exam portals including:

**Medical**
- NEET 2026
- AIIMS
- JIPMER

**Engineering**
- JEE Main 2026
- JEE Advanced
- BITSAT

**Management**
- CAT
- XAT
- SNAP

**Government**
- UPSC
- SSC
- Banking Exams

**Universities**
- CUET
- State University admissions

### What Gets Adapted?

Each portal has specific requirements. We automatically adjust:

| Parameter | What We Do |
|-----------|------------|
| **Dimensions** | Resize to exact pixel requirements (e.g., 400×600) |
| **File Size** | Compress to under the max limit (e.g., 200KB) |
| **Format** | Convert to required format (JPG/PNG) |
| **DPI** | Set correct resolution (e.g., 300 DPI) |
| **Aspect Ratio** | Crop/adjust to match requirements |

### Quality Assurance

After adaptation, you'll see a quality score:
- **Excellent (80%+)** ✅ - Ready for submission
- **Good (50-79%)** ⚠️ - Should work, consider retaking
- **Poor (<50%)** ❌ - Recommend capturing again

## ❓ Troubleshooting

### Camera Not Working

1. Go to Settings → Rythmiq One
2. Enable Camera permission
3. Restart the app

### Upload Taking Too Long

- Check your internet connection
- Try switching from WiFi to mobile data
- Large files take longer - be patient

### Poor Quality Score

- Retake with better lighting
- Ensure document is flat and wrinkle-free
- Clean your camera lens
- Hold camera steady

### Portal Not Listed

Contact us at support@rythmiq.one with:
- Portal name
- Application URL
- Required specifications (if known)

We add new portals regularly!
